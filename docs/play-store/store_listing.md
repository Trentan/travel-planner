# Google Play Store Default Store Listing

This document contains the complete set of text copy and graphics assets required to set up the **Default Store Listing** in the Google Play Console for the Travel Planner app.

---

## 1. Store Copy (Text Assets)

### App Name
* **Limit**: 30 Characters
* **Draft**: `Travel Planner: Offline Route` *(29 characters)*

### Short Description
* **Limit**: 80 Characters
* **Draft**: `Organize your trips, map routes, and manage your budget with offline support.` *(77 characters)*

### Full Description
* **Limit**: 4,000 Characters
* **Draft**:
```text
Travel Planner is your ultimate offline-first companion for designing, mapping, and coordinating your dream itineraries. Whether you are backpacking through Europe or road-tripping cross-country, keep all your travel details organized in a single, high-performance interface.

Key Features:

✈️ Unified Transport & Accommodation
Keep track of flights, trains, rental cars, and hotel reservations in one view. Log booking references (PNRs), departure times, and address details to ensure a frictionless transition between cities.

🗺️ Interactive Maps & Visual Routes
Visualize your journey with clean, color-coded route overlays. View all trip legs, export locations directly to Google Maps, and check statistics on the go.

🗓️ Dynamic Day-by-Day Itineraries
Plan your daily schedules, timelines, and check off sights. Toggle between highly structured "Timeline" views and simplified "Grouped" day layouts to match your pacing.

💰 Budget & Expense Tracker
Monitor travel expenses, convert multiple currencies, and view clear visual KPIs of your spending. Hide prices with a single tap if you're sharing your screen with friends.

🧳 Smart Packing Checklists
Organize your packing lists by category. Ensure you never forget essential travel documents, electronics, or gear before departure.

🤖 AI Prompt Generator
Bootstrap your plan in seconds. Generate customized AI prompts to instantly build outlines for new destinations based on your vibe, duration, and dates.

🔒 Privacy First & Offline-First
Your travel data belongs to you. No cloud sign-ups or logins required. All itineraries are saved securely on your device, ensuring instant access even when you have no signal on flight or rail trips. Easily back up, share, and export your trips with custom privacy presets.
```

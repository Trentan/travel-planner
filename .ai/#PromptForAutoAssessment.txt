7. Apply fixes for all discovered storage, I/O, or onboarding issues. Integrate robust validations into `tests/file-io-robustness-suite.js`.
8. Loop until 0 critical/high issues remain, verifying correctness after each change with `npm test`.

--- PHASE 3: RELEASE VALIDATION ---
9. Switch to the Release Agent role (.ai/RELEASE_AGENT.md).
10. Verify milestone progress and run a full production build locally.
11. Audit against docs/RELEASE_CHECKLIST.md and output docs/RELEASE_REPORT.md with a final GO/NO-GO determination and readiness score.

Do not stop to ask for human confirmation between phases. Proceed continuously until release reports are generated or a fatal execution error occurs.
